## EXHIBIT A — STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 001

**SOW Number:** SOW-001
**SOW Effective Date:** March 15, 2024
**Project Name:** Meridian Core Analytics Platform — Phase 1

### A.1 Project Overview

Service Provider shall design, develop, test, and deploy a cloud-based analytics platform (the "Platform") for Client's internal use, enabling real-time financial reporting, risk monitoring dashboards, and regulatory compliance reporting as further described herein.

### A.2 Scope of Services

Service Provider shall perform the following Services:

1. **Discovery & Requirements Analysis** — Conduct stakeholder interviews, review existing systems and data architecture, and deliver a written requirements specification document within twenty (20) business days of SOW execution.

2. **System Architecture Design** — Design the technical architecture for the Platform, including cloud infrastructure, data pipelines, API specifications, and security framework. Deliverable: Architecture Design Document.

3. **Development — Sprint 1 through Sprint 8** — Develop the Platform in accordance with the requirements specification, using an Agile development methodology. Bi-weekly sprint reviews shall be held with Client stakeholders.

4. **Quality Assurance & Testing** — Conduct unit testing, integration testing, system testing, and user acceptance testing (UAT) in collaboration with Client's designated QA personnel.

5. **Deployment & Go-Live Support** — Deploy the Platform to Client's designated cloud environment and provide on-site go-live support for a period of five (5) business days.

6. **Documentation & Training** — Deliver technical documentation, user manuals, and administer two (2) training sessions for Client's end users.

### A.3 Milestones and Delivery Schedule

| --------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| M-1       | Requirements Specification Document | April 15, 2024     |
| M-2       | Architecture Design Document        | May 1, 2024        |
| M-3       | Sprint 1-4 Demo (Beta Build)        | July 1, 2024       |

| M-5       | UAT Completion Sign-off             | September 30, 2024 |
| M-6       | Production Deployment               | October 15, 2024   |
| M-7       | Documentation & Training            | October 31, 2024   |

### A.4 Fees

- **Fixed-Fee Development:** USD $480,000, payable in monthly installments of USD $60,000 over eight (8) months commencing April 1, 2024.
- **Go-Live Support (T&M):** USD $250 per hour, capped at USD $12,500.
- **Training:** Included in Fixed Fee.
- **Expenses:** Reimbursable per Section 3.6 of the Agreement.

### A.5 Client Responsibilities

Client shall:

- Assign a dedicated Project Manager and at least two (2) subject matter experts to participate in discovery, sprint reviews, and UAT.

- Provision the required cloud infrastructure (AWS or Azure, as agreed) prior to M-3.

## EXHIBIT B — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

### B.1 Applicability

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") applies to the production environment of the Platform following the go-live date (the "Service Commencement Date").

### B.2 Definitions

- **"Uptime"** means the percentage of time in a calendar month during which the Platform is available and operational, excluding Scheduled Maintenance Windows.
- **"Downtime"** means any period during which the Platform is unavailable or materially non-functional, excluding Scheduled Maintenance and Excused Downtime.
- **"Scheduled Maintenance Window"** means maintenance periods scheduled between 12:00 AM and 4:00 AM Eastern Time on Sundays, with at least forty-eight (48) hours' prior written notice to Client.
- **"Excused Downtime"** means downtime attributable to: (i) Force Majeure Events; (ii) Client's acts or omissions; (iii) Third-Party Software failures outside Service Provider's reasonable control; or (iv) Client-initiated changes.

### B.3 Uptime Commitment

| Service Tier             | Monthly Uptime Commitment |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| Core Platform            | 99.5%                     |
| Reporting Module         | 99.0%                     |
| Data Ingestion Pipelines | 99.0%                     |

**Monthly Uptime Calculation:** `((Total Minutes in Month − Downtime Minutes) / Total Minutes in Month) × 100`

### B.4 Incident Response Times

| Priority      | Description                            | Initial Response | Resolution Target |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------- |
| P1 — Critical | Full Platform outage or data loss      | 15 minutes       | 4 hours           |
| P2 — High     | Core feature degraded, no workaround   | 1 hour           | 8 hours           |
| P3 — Medium   | Feature degraded, workaround available | 4 hours          | 2 business days   |
| P4 — Low      | Minor issue, cosmetic, informational   | 1 business day   | 5 business days   |

In the event that Service Provider fails to meet the Uptime Commitment in any calendar month, Client shall be entitled to a service credit as follows:

| Monthly Uptime Achieved | Service Credit     |
| ----------------------- | ------------------ |
| 99.0% – < 99.5%         | 5% of Monthly Fee  |
| 95.0% – < 99.0%         | 10% of Monthly Fee |
| < 95.0%                 | 25% of Monthly Fee |

Service credits are Client's sole and exclusive remedy for Uptime failures and shall be applied as a credit against future invoices. Service credits shall not be applied against any amounts past due. Total service credits in any calendar month shall not exceed twenty-five percent (25%) of the applicable monthly fee.
